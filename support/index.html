Need help with TurnScope? Contact me@tczhong.com.

Importing a recording

Choose English game footage from Files or Photos. Keep TurnScope open while it analyzes the recording. The current import flow analyzes the first battle in a recording.

Fixing an action or unknown HP

Open a battle and select the turn you want to correct. Use the turn editor to edit, add, remove, or reorder individual actions. You can also set starting and ending HP percentages, or leave HP unknown. Review later turns after making a correction.

Writing and finding notes

Open Edit Battle and choose the note button to write your private note for that battle. Open My Notes from the overview to browse and search notes across your battles. Shared reports exclude these notes; journal backups include them.

Purchases and subscriptions

If a purchase is missing, use Restore Purchases while signed in to the Apple Account used for the purchase. Manage subscriptions in your Apple Account settings. For refund requests, use Apple's Report a Problem service.

Reporting an issue

Include the app version, iOS version, device model, and what happened. A short description of the steps helps us reproduce the problem. Please redact private details in screenshots, and avoid sending full recordings or private notes unless requested.

中文使用帮助

导入录像

从文件或照片中选择英文游戏录像，分析时保持 TurnScope 在前台打开。目前导入流程分析录像中的第一场对战。

修改动作与 HP

打开对战并进入需要修改的回合。在回合编辑器中逐条修改、添加、删除或调整动作顺序，也可以填写回合开始和结束的 HP 百分比，或保留未知状态。修改后请检查后续回合。

保存与查看笔记

在编辑对战中点击笔记按钮，每场保存一条私人笔记。概览中的 My Notes 可以浏览和搜索所有对战笔记。分享战报不包含笔记，日志备份包含笔记。

购买与问题反馈

购买权限未显示时，请使用原购买时的 Apple 账户恢复购买。订阅在 Apple 账户设置中管理；退款请求请通过 Apple 的问题报告网站 提交。

反馈时请提供应用版本、iOS 版本、设备型号及操作步骤，截图请先遮挡私人信息。支持邮箱：me@tczhong.com。
