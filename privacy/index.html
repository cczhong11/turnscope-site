Privacy

Your journal is personal.

Privacy policy · Last updated October 7, 2026

This policy explains how TurnScope, developed by Tc Zhong, handles information in the app and on this website. Questions can be sent to me@tczhong.com.

Battle analysis and local storage

TurnScope analyzes imported footage on your device. The app does not upload your recordings, battle data, or notes to our servers. It does not require an app account.

The app stores your journal locally, including recognized player names, teams, actions, HP, outcomes, recording names, timestamps, corrections, and private notes. Recognition may be incomplete or incorrect; you can review and edit the results.

Files and Photos

The app accesses footage you select through the system Files or Photos picker. A Photos import may create a temporary copy for analysis, which the app removes when the import finishes. Importing or deleting a battle in TurnScope does not delete your original recording.

Exports, sharing, and backups

You choose whether to export or share information. Journal backups include private notes and battle data. Shared battle reports exclude private notes, opponent names, source recording names, exact timestamps, and device information. Your own trainer name may be included if you choose.

Exported files, device backups, and storage services such as iCloud are managed by you and their providers. Deleting an entry in TurnScope does not delete copies you have exported or backed up elsewhere.

Purchases

Apple processes purchases through the App Store. TurnScope uses StoreKit to check purchase entitlements and stores the information needed to apply access limits. We do not receive your payment card details.

Tracking and analytics

TurnScope does not use advertising trackers or send app analytics to our servers. It does not sell your battle data or notes.

Support and this website

If you contact support, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, message, or attachments, and use it to respond to your request. Avoid sending private recordings or notes unless they are needed to explain an issue.

This website does not use analytics scripts or advertising cookies. Its hosting and delivery providers may process standard request information, such as IP addresses and browser information, to serve and secure the website.

Your choices

You can edit or delete battles and notes in the app, and keep your own exported backups. You can also remove the app's local data by deleting the app. Copies held in device backups or external storage may remain under those services' controls. Deleting the app does not cancel an App Store subscription.

Policy updates

We will update this page when information handling changes and revise the date above.

中文版隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 7 日。TurnScope 由 Tc Zhong 开发。隐私问题请联系 me@tczhong.com。

本地分析与存储

录像分析在设备上进行。应用不会把录像、对战数据或笔记上传到我们的服务器，也不要求注册应用账户。本地日志包含识别出的玩家名称、队伍、动作、HP、胜负、录像名称、时间、手动修正和私人笔记。识别结果可能不完整或不准确，你可以逐条修正。

录像访问

应用通过系统文件或照片选择器访问你选择的录像。照片导入可能生成临时副本，导入结束后由应用删除。在 TurnScope 中导入或删除对战不会删除原始录像。

导出、分享与备份

导出和分享由你主动选择。日志备份包含私人笔记和对战数据；分享战报不包含私人笔记、对手名称、原始录像名称、精确时间或设备信息，你可以选择是否包含自己的训练家名称。导出的文件、设备备份及 iCloud 等服务由你与相应服务商管理。删除应用中的数据不会删除外部副本。

购买与追踪

购买由 Apple 通过 App Store 处理，应用通过 StoreKit 检查购买权限并保存实施使用限制所需的信息。我们不会收到银行卡信息。应用不使用广告追踪器，不向我们的服务器发送应用分析数据，也不出售你的对战数据或笔记。

支持与网站

联系支持时，我们会收到你主动发送的邮箱、信息和附件，并用于处理你的请求。除非确有必要，请不要发送私人录像或笔记。网站不使用分析脚本或广告 Cookie，托管和内容分发服务商可能为提供及保护网站处理 IP 地址、浏览器信息等基本请求数据。

管理你的数据

你可以在应用中修改或删除对战与笔记，或导出自己的备份。删除应用可移除应用的本地数据；设备备份或外部存储中的副本需在对应服务中管理。删除应用不会取消 App Store 订阅。本政策变更时会更新本页面及日期。
